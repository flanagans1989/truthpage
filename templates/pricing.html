What happens after the 14-day trial?

You'll be prompted to add a payment method to keep your monitoring running. Nothing is charged automatically before then — no card is required to start the trial.

What if I need to monitor more than 25 pages?

How does the AI tell material changes from cosmetic ones?

Every detected diff is classified by AI as material or cosmetic. Cosmetic changes (typos, formatting) publish automatically; anything that could affect your customers goes to your approval queue first — you always have the final say before it reaches your trust page.
