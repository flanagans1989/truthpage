How this works

We hash the file you upload and compare it to the digest in manifest.txt, then — if the pack claims an independent timestamp — check the .tsr token against TrustPages' own trusted timestamp-authority certificate, never a CA bundled inside your upload (that would let anyone fake a "verified" result with their own throwaway certificate). A pack with no timestamp isn't a failure — most packs won't have one until they're regenerated after this feature shipped. You can run the same check fully offline with the verify.sh script included in every evidence ZIP.
