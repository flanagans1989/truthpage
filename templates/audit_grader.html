We fetch the page you paste and check it against our directory of 32 commonly-used vendors — no AI, no guessing, name matches only. The grade reflects how many known sub-processors were found; it always caps below A, because finding a vendor's name on a page is not the same as monitoring that page for changes or holding dated, cryptographically verifiable evidence of what it said before.
